Our Philosophy

Why Crumb & Co. Tastes Better

We refuse to compromise on quality, process, or time.

🧈

High-Fat Cultured Butter

We use 84% butterfat European-style butter brown-steeped daily. This creates rich caramel notes and that unmistakable soft middle.

⏱️

48-Hour Cold Fermentation

Dough rests in cold aging for two full days before hitting the oven. This develops complex flavor structure and ultimate chew.

🍫

Hand-Chopped Chocolate

No uniform chips here. We hand-chop whole blocks of 70% Valrhona dark chocolate for puddles of melted cacao in every single bite.

Pro Warm-Up Guide

Recreate the warm bakery oven feel at home

Conventional Oven (Best):

Preheat to 350°F (175°C). Place cookies on baking sheet for 3-4 minutes until chocolate glistens.

Microwave (Quick):

Microwave on medium power for 10-12 seconds alongside a moist paper towel to preserve soft center.
